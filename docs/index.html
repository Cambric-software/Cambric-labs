What You Can Do

Build Neural Networks

📊

Visualize Learning

Save Projects

Generate Python or JavaScript code from trained networks.

How Cambric Labs Works

Your Device

Your CPU, RAM, and storage

↓
Cambric Labs App

Downloaded and installed locally

↓
Local Neural Engine

All computation happens on your device

↓
Your Data Stays Local

Projects, datasets, and trained models never leave your device

No server costs for training. Cambric Labs runs entirely on your device. Training a neural network uses your CPU, not Cambric's servers.

Getting Started

1

Download

Get the Windows installer or Android APK above. Installation takes less than a minute.

2

Create

Build your first network. Add layers, choose activation functions, create a dataset.

3

Train

Watch your network learn. Step through training one cycle at a time or run full epochs.

4

Inspect

See exactly what neurons, weights, and gradients are doing at every step.

5

Export

Export your trained model or generate code for use in your own projects.
